# Design Principles

These principles are as important as the feature list. Any future UI work should stay in this spirit.

## 1. No likes, no like counts, no follower counts

Social signal comes from actions with meaning: shelving a book, leaving a review, picking a favourite. There are no like buttons anywhere in the app. This is permanently out of scope.

## 2. No streaks pushed at the user

Consistency data exists in the insights tab, but the app never nags and never sends "don't break your streak" notifications. Reading should feel like a choice, not an obligation.

## 3. No infinite scroll

Activity feeds are reverse-chronological and paginate with a clear "load more" button. No endless feed. The user reads at their own pace.

## 4. Slow interactions

Logging a book is a deliberate, slightly tactile flow, not a single tap. The multi-step sheet (search, select, rate, review, shelf) is intentional. It should feel like writing in a journal.

## 5. Covers and typography do the visual work

No emoji reactions, no stickers, no confetti animations. Book covers, serif type, and warm colours carry the aesthetic. The app should feel like a bookshelf, not a social feed.

## 6. Reviews are the primary social currency

Short, honest sentences. The UI rewards writing a real thought, not writing for engagement. Review placeholders encourage brevity: "A sentence or two is plenty."

## 7. Dark mode from day one

Readers read at night. The dark mode uses warm tones (deep aubergine-brown, cream text, coral accents) rather than cold blue-black.

## 8. No em dashes in any UI copy

Use commas, semicolons, "and", or sentence breaks. This applies to empty states, buttons, placeholders, and all microcopy. Strict rule.

## Visual Identity

- **Colour palette**: Warm and light with a faded-photograph feel
  - Background: soft cream `#FAF4E8`
  - Text: warm dark brown `#3A2E1F`
  - Primary accent: muted coral/terracotta `#D98872`
  - Secondary accent: mustardy ochre `#C9A961`
  - Borders: gentle sand `#EADFC8`
  - Dark mode: deep aubergine-brown `#2A221C`, cream text `#F0E6D2`
- **Typography**: Serif (Lora) for book titles, reviews, and literary content. Sans (Inter) for UI chrome.
- **Feel**: A worn paperback, a dim bar, a polaroid from the 90s. Warm, a bit wistful, lived-in.
